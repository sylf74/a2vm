# dispatch — pillar 4, Dispatch & Capitalization

Carries the decisions of pillar 3 to the tools of the teams that act on them, and brings their
state back. v0.20.0: remediation tickets in the ITSM (iTop), one per asset and fix, followed until
a scan confirms the fix; requests for remediation by a person from a dashboard (D18).

- Operation: `docs/dispatch.md`
